01 About

The short version

I'm Yash Thakur, a first-year student in Pune. I value being someone a team can rely on: listening carefully, communicating clearly, and taking responsibility for my part of the work. I’m interested in roles where I can connect with people and help turn a shared idea into something real.

As a class monitor through school and Class Representative in Class 12, I learned how much of leadership is simply being reachable and doing what you said you would. Alongside college, I've been building an art page from scratch: proof that I can grow an audience without a budget, using consistency and a bit of design instinct.

I'm looking for a club where that mix is useful — a team that needs someone to handle PR and social media, support events, and occasionally make the thing look better.

— Yash
